Shipping Policy

1. Shipping Rates

Shipping rates are calculated based on the weight of your order and your location.

2. Shipping Times

Orders are typically processed, shipped and delivered within 5-7 business days.

3. International Shipping

We offer international shipping to select countries. Please note that customs duties and taxes may apply. Orders will be delivered within 10-15 business days.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order has shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email or SMS.
